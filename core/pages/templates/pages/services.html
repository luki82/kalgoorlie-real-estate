Drafting for council approval

From a new patio to a full custom home, I prepare the plans you need to lodge with council, and make the changes council asks for along the way.

What I draft

Not sure where your project fits? Send me the details and I'll let you know.

Small jobs

Outdoor structures added to an existing home.

  • Patios and pergolas
  • Carports
  • Sheds and garages
  • Alfrescos and decks

Medium jobs

Extra dwellings and drawings of existing buildings.

  • Granny flats
  • Plans of an existing house (e.g. for strata titles)
  • Concept floor plans for a new home

Large jobs

Changes to your home, new houses and commercial buildings.

  • Extensions and renovations
  • Full plans for a new custom home
  • Small to mid-size commercial buildings

What you get

  • Site plan, floor plans, elevations and sections (as your project needs)
  • Drawn to the National Construction Code and Australian Standards
  • PDF plans ready to lodge with council
  • Changes made when the council planner requests them

Good to know before you start

My quotes cover drafting only. Depending on your project, you may also need:

  • a land survey of your property
  • a structural engineer to certify footings or structural changes
  • an energy efficiency assessment (usually for new homes and extensions)
  • council application fees

I'll tell you up front which of these apply to your project, so there are no surprises.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your project. Send the property address and what you want to build. Sketches or photos help.
  2. Get a quote. Including which surveys, engineering or assessments your project needs.
  3. Review the drawings. We go over the plans together until you're happy with them.
  4. Lodge with council. If the planner asks for changes, I update the plans until they're approved.
